The labels in these examples only show the layout. They are not the answers.

  • Header row with exactly these three column names: id, type, severity. The order of the columns does not matter. Other columns are ignored.
  • 300 rows: ids 1 to 300, each id once.
  • Upper or lower case and extra spaces do not matter (near miss = Near miss), but the spelling must match.

type must be one of:

InjuryFireReleaseEquipmentNear miss

severity must be one of:

HighMediumLow

3. Check your answers

How you are scored

  • Type % and Severity %: the share of the 300 reports where that column is right.
  • Pass: both Type and Severity are 95% or more. Excellent: both are 99% or more.
  • The checker lists the ids where at least one column is wrong. It does not say which column or what the right label is: go back to the rules in the problem statement.
  • Every check is recorded for your teacher (student ID, name, time and score).
